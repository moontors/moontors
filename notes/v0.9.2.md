Moontors 0.9.2

Moontors, the agentic development environment (ADE) for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok Build and OpenCode, still runs Claude Code 2.1.332, Codex 0.172.0, Gemini CLI 0.71.1, Grok Build 1.0.92 and OpenCode 2.4.2. This release lets your phone pair with several of your computers and switch between them, and turns the arcs of the phone's moon while it connects.

What's new:

- **Your phone pairs with several of your computers** and shows one computer's board at a time: the one you used last, as the app opens. The board's top names the computer it shows, after **Moontors**; choose it for **Your computers**, which lists each computer the phone keeps, the one shown marked **Linked now**. **Switch** opens another computer's board, and **Forget** forgets that computer alone, asking first. **Pair another computer** pairs one more while the phone stays on the board it's showing, **Back to** that computer's name going back to its board as you left it; once paired, the phone opens the new computer's board. What you typed and didn't send stays with each computer's own sessions, there again when you switch back. Each computer you keep still sends its notifications, and a tap on one opens that session on its computer, the phone switching to it first. Where the phone can't reach a computer and keeps another, the card offers **Switch computer**. Pairing a computer the phone already keeps pairs nothing again: the phone says it already has that computer, and **Open its board** opens it. See [More than one computer](https://moontors.com/docs/phone/#more-than-one-computer).

What's changed:

- **While your phone connects to your computer**, as it opens, comes back after a while away, or reaches your computer again, directly or through the relay, the moon's two arcs turn around it, as bright as ever, and the moon is dimmed. Once it's connected, the arcs come to rest where they always stand and the moon is bright again. Where your phone asks for less motion, the arcs stay still and the moon still dims. See [The board](https://moontors.com/docs/phone/#the-board).
- **The time tracker's foot says you can tell your agents to start a timer**, beside the list's total, on the current list, in the window and on your phone. See [Agents and the time tracker](https://moontors.com/docs/time-tracker/#agents-and-the-time-tracker).
- Moontors runs on Electron 45.4.1.

What's fixed:

- **A phone you Revoke under Phones on your account** for another of your computers is unpaired there even when that computer is asleep or offline, once both computers run 0.9.2, the one you revoke from and the one that was away: at once while it's connected, and otherwise as it next reaches Moontors' server signed in to your account, as when it wakes, comes back online or signs in again, whether or not your plan still includes the relay. Until then, no computer gives that phone its relay access back. It used to stay paired there, and could get its relay access again the next time it reached that computer. See [Reaching your computer from anywhere](https://moontors.com/docs/moontors-account/#reaching-your-computer-from-anywhere).
- **On macOS, a click on the window's top bar**, where **Quick feedback** sits, closes a menu or a dropdown such as the time tracker, as a click anywhere else in the window does, where it left them open. That click doesn't move the window, and the next drags it. What you close yourself, such as a page from the header, stays open. See [The top bar](https://moontors.com/docs/window/#the-top-bar).
- On the iPhone, a swipe home that starts over a board card no longer opens that card or its actions, so the app no longer comes back inside its transcript: nothing a touch does acts while the app isn't in front.

This version updates the Android app and the iPhone app. The Windows and Linux builds still aren't signed, so Windows asks once before Moontors opens. The README's "First launch" and "Updates" sections say what to expect on each platform.

Found a problem, or have an idea? Send it from **Quick feedback**, or open an issue: https://github.com/moontors/moontors/issues/new/choose
