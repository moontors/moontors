Moontors 0.8.8

Moontors, the agentic development environment (ADE) for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok Build and OpenCode, still runs Claude Code 2.1.325, Codex 0.169.0, Gemini CLI 0.68.0, Grok Build 1.0.77 and OpenCode 2.3.0. This release fixes how Moontors and its Android app connect on networks that inspect HTTPS, as many companies' networks do.

What's fixed:

- On a network that inspects HTTPS with a certificate of its own, as many companies' networks do, Moontors couldn't reach its server: Profile said "Couldn't reach Moontors' server", and your Moontors account, Pro, settings sync, the relay to your phone and its notifications didn't work there. Moontors now trusts the certificates your computer trusts, as your browser does, on macOS, Windows and Linux, and those in the files your shell names in `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS` and `SSL_CERT_FILE`. A network that lets Moontors out only through a proxy you name still blocks those connections for now. See [Known limits](https://moontors.com/docs/known-limits/#platforms).
- On Android, the phone reaches your computer through the relay on a network that inspects HTTPS, once that network's certificate is installed on the phone, as a company's phones have it: the app trusts that certificate for Moontors' server alone, and the link inside stays encrypted to your computer. On a Wi-Fi that sets a proxy, the phone now reaches your computer's own addresses directly, where the proxy kept every link from opening. See [Reaching it from anywhere](https://moontors.com/docs/phone/#reaching-it-from-anywhere).

This version updates the Android app; the iPhone app isn't updated this time. The Windows and Linux builds still aren't signed, so Windows asks once before Moontors opens. The README's "First launch" and "Updates" sections say what to expect on each platform.

Found a problem, or have an idea? Open an issue: https://github.com/moontors/moontors/issues/new/choose
