Moontors 0.8.12

Moontors, the agentic development environment (ADE) for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok Build and OpenCode, now runs Claude Code 2.1.328, Codex 0.170.0, Gemini CLI 0.69.0, Grok Build 1.0.89 and OpenCode 2.3.2. This release adds Notes, your own plain text beside your sessions, and lets each agent keep several accounts you switch between.

What's new:

- **Notes**: a view on the rail, beside Git, Files and Terminal, with three notes side by side: **Global**, shared by every project; **Project**, shared by the project's folder and its worktrees; and **Session**, the focused session's own, or the project's own while no session is focused. Each saves itself as you type and shows at once in every window and on your phone. Drag the line between two columns to widen one and narrow the other; the widths are kept for each project. On the phone, a session's header opens its three notes, and the board's top row opens Global. See [Notes](https://moontors.com/docs/notes/).
- **Several accounts per agent**: **Switch account** on Providers lists the agent's other saved accounts, each named as the agent reports it, and a new sign-in. Every session moves to the account you pick once it's at rest. A saved account is checked before the switch, and one whose sign-in has expired asks you to sign in again. You can sign in to another account ahead without switching to it, and signing in again to a saved account never adds a second row. **Remove an account** signs out of one you no longer need and forgets it, asking first; the account in use goes only by signing out. On the phone, **Switch** on the Accounts sheet switches too. See [Several accounts](https://moontors.com/docs/sign-in/#several-accounts).
- **OpenCode's prices**: under each model's name, OpenCode's model menu says **Free**, or what the model costs per 1M tokens, and an OpenCode Go model's price as the plan's allowance.
- **OpenCode's plan and limits** on its account card, as Claude Code's and Codex's show theirs: OpenCode Go's plan with its 5-hour, weekly and monthly windows, and the OpenCode Console workspace's spend budgets.

What's changed:

- Claude Code 2.1.328, Codex 0.170.0, Gemini CLI 0.69.0, Grok Build 1.0.89 and OpenCode 2.3.2:
  - With Grok Build, `/models` reads the agent's model list again.
  - With Claude Code, **Reload connectors** on the MCP list connects a claude.ai connector you just added without a new session, a note says when Claude Code held another session's message back, and its note of a connector's saved files links each file.
  - With OpenCode, a command it moves to the background shows as a background task until it ends.
- Moontors keeps the agents' API keys and cloud sign-ins, your MCP servers' values, your Moontors account and each paired phone's secret in its own encrypted store, with one key in the system keychain, on macOS, Windows and Linux alike. Its first start moves what the keychain held.
- The model menu's rows: each model's name with its marks on the first line, what it says and costs under it across the row, and the check mark in a column of its own, on your computer and your phone. A mark every model in the menu has, such as Claude's reasoning, is no longer drawn.
- Codex's commands read as the other agents' do: one that only reads files shows as a read, and one that only searches as a search, titled by its file or what it looks for; any other by the script it runs.
- An agent's message to another of your sessions (`send_to_session`) no longer asks for approval, with any agent and in any mode. When Codex's auto-review denies an MCP tool's call, its card offers **Approve once**.

What's fixed:

- A key or sign-in your shell sets for Codex or Grok Build, or an access token for Gemini CLI's Google sign-in, no longer takes over the account you signed in with in Moontors.
- Your shell's `OPENCODE_API_KEY` no longer reaches Moontors' OpenCode. If your OpenCode Console or Go models ran on it, sign in with it in Moontors: paste it into **OpenCode Console API key**.
- A new OpenCode home no longer takes in your own OpenCode sign-ins or sessions from your computer.
- OpenCode now offers **Sign out**, as its guide said.

This version updates the Android app; the iPhone app isn't updated this time. The Windows and Linux builds still aren't signed, so Windows asks once before Moontors opens. The README's "First launch" and "Updates" sections say what to expect on each platform.
