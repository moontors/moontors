Moontors 0.9.0

Moontors, the agentic development environment (ADE) for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok Build and OpenCode, still runs Claude Code 2.1.330, Codex 0.171.0, Gemini CLI 0.70.0, Grok Build 1.0.92 and OpenCode 2.3.2, now on Electron 45.3.4. This release adds a time tracker, on your computer and your phone, and takes a notification away once it no longer needs you.

What's new:

- **The time tracker**: the clock in the window's header times the tasks you work on, several at once if you like, each with its name, its project and your notes. Moontors' background service keeps it, so a timer counts on with the window closed, while the computer sleeps and across a restart, and the clock turns blue while one runs. Archive a list under a name and start an empty one, restore an archived list, sort the list, and export it as a CSV file. Your phone has the same lists, from the clock at the top right of its board. Ask a session's agent to start or stop a task's timer, and it does, through Moontors' own tools, without asking first. See [The time tracker](https://moontors.com/docs/time-tracker/).
- **Notifications go once they no longer need you**, on your computer and your phone alike. "Waiting for you" goes once the wait is over, answered here or on your phone, or the agent moved on. An error's or a finished session's goes once you've looked at the session or it runs again, and a group's once you've looked at any of its sessions. Archiving a session takes its notification with it. An iPhone takes one away when iOS lets Moontors run in the background, and otherwise as you open the app. See [Notifications](https://moontors.com/docs/sessions-notifications/#notifications).

What's changed:

- **Claude Code's `/loop` and scheduled jobs outlive a restart**: when Moontors' background service stops, Claude Code ends by itself, or you `/restart` the session, Moontors starts Claude Code again with no message, Claude Code sets its jobs again, and the card stays **Scheduled**. `/restart` keeps them without asking. Archiving the session ends them. A **Scheduled** session, or a running time tracker timer, keeps the background service running with the window closed; the runs missed while it was stopped aren't made up. See [Keep agents running when the window closes](https://moontors.com/docs/how-to/keep-agents-running-when-the-window-closes/).
- On a long session, **Approve once** and the other actions that look back through it read only as far as they need, never the whole session, so they no longer hold up every session for a moment, or take about 250 MB, on the longest.
- When the part of Moontors that runs your sessions stops, the logs say when and how: the exit code or the signal that ended it, and how long it ran. What it printed as it stopped stays on your computer, in `~/.moontors/daemon-output.log`, and never goes with a **Quick feedback** report, as it can hold your prompts' words or your files' paths.
- Moontors runs on Electron 45.3.4.
- **The iPhone app is out**, on the App Store: the Phones dialog offers it beside the Android app on Google Play, as moontors.com does.

What's fixed:

- A card's **Action denied** on a long session lands on the denied call's row once the session has loaded from its start, on your computer and your phone, never in the transcript's middle.
- OpenCode's and Grok Build's token counts include all they use, as every other agent's do: OpenCode's tokens in include its cache's reads and writes, and both agents' tokens out include their thinking.
- An API key's sign-in and **This project**'s MCP scope say Moontors keeps them encrypted in its own store, where they named the keychain.
- A long session opens on its newest turns, and the row you're reading stays where it is as its earlier turns load in above it, where a scroll in that moment could leave you in the transcript's middle.

This version updates the Android app and the iPhone app. The Windows and Linux builds still aren't signed, so Windows asks once before Moontors opens. The README's "First launch" and "Updates" sections say what to expect on each platform.

Found a problem, or have an idea? Send it from **Quick feedback**, or open an issue: https://github.com/moontors/moontors/issues/new/choose
